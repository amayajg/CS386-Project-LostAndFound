## 1. System Description

<!-- Intro paragraph will be added here -->

The problem of an inefficient campus lost and found system affects NAU students and faculty members who lose personal or academic items all over campus, forcing them to waste time physically searching for their lost items or outright giving them up. The NAU Lost & Found System is a web-based lost and found management app that allows people all over campus to efficiently search for and report lost belongings; unlike a traditional lost and found, which generally requires students to visit multiple locations or contact staff individually, our product will provide a standardized system for reporting and searching for lost belongings. The NAU Lost and Found System helps NAU students and staff recover lost belongings without building-by-building guesswork of typical lost and found desks. Instead of running all over campus, the web app automatically matches your lost item report against items already turned in and notify you, while the rest of the inventory remains private.

### Log Found Item (Brennan)

The system allows a **Staff Member** to log a **Found Item** so that it can later be matched against reports from people who lost something. A **Staff Member** **_logs_** each Found Item after recieving or discovering it. Each **Found Item** has a *description*, a *date_found*, a *location_found*, a *status* (such as unclaimed or matched), and a *holding_location* indictating where the item is currently kept. A found item may also **_include_** zero or more **Photos**, each of which has an *image_file*. The staff member submits this information through a **Found Item Report**, which **_records_** exactly one **Found Item**. The system **_validates_** the report before it is accepted.

### Search by Location

<!-- Amaya -->
A **Campus** User can **_search by_** a Location to find lost or found items in a specific area on campus. Each **Location** has a _name_ and _description_ to identify the building or area where an item was lost or found. The system shows matching **Lost Item Reports** and **Found Item Reports** based on the location entered, making it easier for users to find their missing items.

### Report Lost Item

<!-- Evelyn -->
A **Student** can **_submit_** a **Lost Item Report** with details like _category_, _color_, _date_, and _location_. The system will check for a duplicate of *Lost Item Report* based off the search keywords. The system must show results within 10 seconds. The users should be able to create a report in 5 minutes or less. 

### View Map of Items

A **Community Member** can view a map of campus, showing nearby **Lost Item**s matching a given _description_ and _location_. Most **Item**s are **_located in_** specific **Building**s, which also causes those **Building**s' _front desk location_ to be displayed. The map includes **Building**s on campus, with their _numbers_ and
_addresses_, as well as locations where students frequently lose items like **Bus Stop**s and **Benches**. Certain buildings may have a **floor plan** overlaid on their
outline, allowing **Community Members** to see the _front desk location_ relative to internal **Landmark**s, like a central hallway, a water fountain, or a certain entrance.
