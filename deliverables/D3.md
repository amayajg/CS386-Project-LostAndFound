## 1. System Description

The problem of an inefficient campus lost and found system affects NAU students and faculty members who lose personal or academic items all over campus, forcing them to waste time physically searching for their lost items or outright giving them up. The NAU Lost and Found System is a web-based lost and found management app that allows people all over campus to efficiently search for and report lost belongings; unlike a traditional lost and found, which generally requires students to visit multiple locations or contact staff individually, our product will provide a standardized system for reporting and searching for lost belongings. The NAU Lost and Found System helps NAU students and staff recover lost belongings without building-by-building guesswork of typical lost and found desks. Instead of running all over campus, the web app automatically matches your lost item report against items already turned in and notify you, while the rest of the inventory remains private.

### Search for Lost Item (Lareine)

A **Community Member** has a _name_ and _email_. They can search for a **Found Item Report** using details like _category_, _color_, _date found_, and a **Location**. The system shows matching **Found Item Reports** based on the search keywords. Users can review the results to see if any found items match what they are looking for. If there are no matching results, the system will let the user know.

### Log Found Item (Brennan)

The system allows a **Staff Member** to **_log_** a **Found Item Report** so that it can later be matched against reports from people who lost something. A **Staff Member** is a kind of **Community Member** with a _staff id_ who can log a **Found Item Report** after receiving or discovering it. Each **Found Item Report** is **_found at_** a **Location** with a _category_, _color_, and _date found_ as well as a _submitted at_ timestamp, _description_, _status_ (unclaimed or claimed), and a _holding location_ (where it is currently being kept, which might not match the **Location** it was found at). A **Found Item Report** may also **_include_** zero or more **Photos**, each of which has an _image file_. After a report is submitted, the system checks the **Found Item Report** against existing **Lost Item Reports**. Each **Lost Item Report** is **_submitted by_** a **Community Member** and is **_lost at_** a **Location**. The report has a _description_, _date lost_, _category_, and _color_. When the system finds a possible match, it creates a **Match** that **_links_** one **Found Item Report** to one **Lost Item Report** with a _match status_ (such as possible, confirmed, or rejected) and a _created at_ date. A **Found Item Report** and **Lost Item Report** could both have many **Matches** if the reports look similar. When a **Match** is made, a **Notification** is **_sent to_** the **Community Member** who submitted the **Lost Item Report**. Each **Notification** **_refers to_** one **Match** and has a _message_ and a _sent at_ timestamp. If no match is found, the **Found Item Report** keeps the _status_ "unclaimed" so it remains open for future matches. No **Notification** is sent.

### Search by Location (Amaya)

A **Community Member** can search by a **Location** to find **Lost Item Reports** or **Found Item Reports** in a specific area on campus. Each **Location** has a _name_ and _description_ to identify the building or area where an item was lost or found. The system shows matching **Lost Item Reports** and **Found Item Reports** based on the location entered, making it easier for users to find their missing items.

### Report Lost Item (Evelyn)

Each **Lost Item Report** is **_submitted by_** a **Community Member**. The report has a _description_, _category_, _color_, and _date lost_. Each report is **_lost at_** a **Location**. The system will check for existing **Lost Item Reports** that match on their _description_, _category_, _color_, and _date lost_ to avoid reporting duplicates. The system must show results within 10 seconds. The users should be able to create a report in 5 minutes or less. 

### View Map of Items (Rose)

A **Community Member** can view a map of campus, showing nearby **Lost Item Reports** matching a given _description_ and **Location**. A **Building** is a kind of **Location**. The map has **Building**s on campus, with their _number_, _address_, and _front desk location_. It also has **Locations** where students frequently lose items like Bus Stops and Benches. A **Building** may **_contain_** a **Floor Plan** which **_contains_** **Landmarks**. Each **Landmark** has a _name_, such as a central hallway, a water fountain, or a particular entrance so that users can find the front desk relative to it.
